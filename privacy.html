Privacy notice

This starter website does not store form submissions on a server. The enquiry form opens the visitor's email application, where the visitor can review and send the message.

Before enabling a database, member accounts, donations, analytics or file uploads, the Trust should publish a complete privacy notice describing what information is collected, why it is needed, who can access it, how long it is retained, and how users can request correction or deletion.

Do not send sensitive identity documents, bank details, passwords, UPI PINs or OTPs through this website. Update this notice with the Trust's approved contact and legal information before launch.
