Who we are

Care that creates opportunity.

Annai Women's Charitable Trust is committed to supporting women, children and vulnerable members of our community. We aim to connect people with practical help, knowledge, skills and supportive networks.

Our approach is rooted in dignity, inclusion, transparency and respect. We welcome volunteers, members, donors and community partners who share this purpose.

  • Women’s welfare and safety awareness
  • Education and scholarship assistance
  • Vocational training and self-employment support
  • Social service for children and senior citizens
